01 / Introduction

Designing with what the city already has.

I am an architect and urban designer exploring how inherited places can support more sustainable, inclusive and culturally grounded urban futures.

My work moves between architectural design, urban research and environmental policy. I am particularly interested in adaptive reuse, railway and industrial heritage, circular regeneration, climate-neutral cities and environmental justice. Across these fields, I examine how spatial transformation can respond not only to environmental targets, but also to community needs, cultural identity and everyday urban life.
