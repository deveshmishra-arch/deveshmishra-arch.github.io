01 / Introduction

Designing with what the city already has.

I am an architect and urban designer exploring how inherited places can support more sustainable, inclusive and culturally grounded urban futures.

My work moves between architectural design, urban research and environmental policy. I am particularly interested in adaptive reuse, railway and industrial heritage, circular regeneration, climate-neutral cities and environmental justice.

Across these fields, I examine how spatial transformation can respond not only to environmental targets, but also to community needs, cultural identity and everyday urban life.

03 / Selected work

From research and analysis to spatial design.

01

Circular Adaptive Reuse of Manchester Mayfield Depot

Research → Analysis → Design

An investigation of railway heritage, urban disconnection and resource flows leading to a regenerative framework connecting cultural programmes, ecological systems and circular design strategies.

02

Regenerative Landscapes of the Aurelian Walls

Research → Analysis → Design

A multiscalar reading of historic infrastructure and its contemporary urban landscape, developed into a proposal treating heritage as a living environmental and civic system.

03

Climate City Contracts and Environmental Justice

Policy research → Comparative analysis → Spatial framework

A developing inquiry into who participates in—and who benefits from—Europe’s urban climate transition, with particular attention to vulnerable communities and unequal employment transitions.
